### Short description

### Full description

FieldProof 360 Pro helps field professionals create clear, professional proof-of-work reports.

Document service calls, inspections, maintenance visits, installations, and repairs from one app. Build reports with customer details, job notes, equipment information, diagnosis, work performed, materials, photos, and signatures. Finalize the report and share a polished PDF when the job is done.

FEATURES

• Create and manage customer records.
• Draft service reports for field work and inspections.
• Add before, during, and after photos from your camera or photo library.
• Capture technician and customer signatures.
• Record materials used.
• Finalize reports with permanent report numbers.
• Preview, export, and share professional PDFs.
• Keep V1 report data on your device.

PLANS

The Free plan includes up to 3 finalized reports per calendar month.

FieldProof 360 Pro provides unlimited finalized reports and removes FieldProof 360 Pro branding from PDFs. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Google Play. Prices are shown by Google Play before purchase. Restore eligible purchases and manage your subscription from the app.

### Data stored only locally — do not mark as collected

The following are stored only on the device in V1 and are not transmitted by the app. Do not add them to Data Safety as collected or shared:

- Customer names and contact/business details
- Service reports and report fields
- Report photos
- Technician and customer signatures
- Materials, report numbers, and generated PDFs

### Data not collected or shared

Do not select these data types for the current app: location, contacts, SMS/MMS, call logs, email/text-message content, photos or videos, files and documents, audio files, health data, browsing history, search history, app activity, crash logs, diagnostics, advertising ID, and other device identifiers.

### Verification before submitting

RevenueCat’s guidance requires Purchase history to be disclosed and recommends the purposes **App functionality** and **Analytics**. Confirm in the RevenueCat dashboard that there are no advertising, attribution, analytics, or other third-party integrations enabled. If a custom app user ID or customer attributes such as email or phone are ever sent to RevenueCat, update this declaration.
